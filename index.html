01 // The Derby

Built from scrap.
Run until it dies.

Scavengers from across the Western Reaches strip, weld and hot-wire whatever the desert left behind, then throw it into the pit. Three classes, one rule: the last engine running takes the Scrapyard Cup.

I

Junker Class

Rust-bucket sedans and welded wagons. Stripped glass, chained doors, and spikes on every bumper.

II

Heavy Haulers

Salvaged trucks and haulers with exposed pipes and steam stacks. Slow to turn, brutal on impact.

III

Scrapyard Final

Winners from every heat meet in a single arena. Neon-lit, steam-choked, no second chances.

03 // Find Us

Easy to find.
Hard to leave.

Directions

Niima Outpost Speedway sits 2 miles past Cantina Plaza. Turn left at the Moisture Farms and follow the smoke.

Parking

Park in the Docking Bay 94 lot. Overflow parking is in the salvage yard behind the grandstand.
